#### **Definitions**

This module shows the Annualized and Accumulated change. 

**Annualized Change**

This the average change per year. Change is expressed in % canopy cover/year (canopy cover) or units of the spectral index/year (canopy moisture, productivity). We calculate annual rates of change for the pre-intervention period, post-intervention period, and the full time series. Additionally, you may view how much the annual rates of change differ before and after an intervention (“delta”). 

**Accumulated Change**

This is the total change that has occurred. Change is expressed in % canopy cover/time period (canopy cover) or units of the spectral index/time period (canopy moisture, productivity). We calculate the total change for the pre-intervention period, post-intervention period, and the full time series. Additionally, you may view how much the total change differs before and after an intervention (“delta”). 

#### **How to Use**

In this module you can view modeled vegetation change. Start by selecting an island, a metric of interest, a time period, and a probability level. The left side of the map will display all pixels that have been modeled for that metric. The right side of the map will display only the pixels that are significant at your desired probability level. The map will display on the right. Select a pixel on the map to see a pixel-level timeseries plot below. 

#### **Interpretation**

This leaflet map illustrates how vegetation is changing each year. Positive values mean the vegetation index is increasing (more canopy cover, canopy moisture, productivity) while negative values indicate the index is decreasing through time. Small values close to zero will indicate a smaller magnitude of change, and larger values indicate larger magnitude of change.  

If a pixel is present on the left side of the leaflet map but does not appear on the right side, this means that the annual/accumulated change was not significant at the chosen probability level.
