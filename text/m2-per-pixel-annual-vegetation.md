#### **Definition**

These are **model predictions** of the yearly averages for canopy cover, canopy moisture, and productivity for each pixel that met our criteria for analysis. We use model predictions because this method allows us to reduce external interference. 

#### **How to Use**

In this module you can compare modeled annual vegetation values between years. Start by selecting an island and a metric of interest. The first year you select will display on the left side of the map. The second year you select will display on the right side of the map. Please note that not all years for all islands for all metrics have been modeled yet. The map will display on the right. Select a pixel on the map to see a pixel-level timeseries below. 

#### **Interpretation**

The pixel-level timeseries shows the average trend (thick, black line) and the gray shaded area around it indicates the range of likely values, indicating an 85% certainty about the trend. Dots are the actual measurements estimated from the satellite images. There may be an orange dashed vertical line, which marks the year of intervention that we modeled. This figure includes two panels: (a, left) shows the smoothed trend, which reduces background noise and variation to show the underlying pattern of change, and (b, right) shows the trend with annual variation. In some instances, panels a and b may be quite similar, indicating minimal interannual variation at that location; in other instances, these panels will be very different, highlighting lots of interannual variation.
