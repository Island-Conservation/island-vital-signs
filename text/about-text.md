Island Vital Signs supports climate-resilient restoration planning by visualizing ecosystem recovery trends at high spatial resolution. Use it to identify areas of strong recovery, guide rewilding, and prioritize future interventions.

### **Methods & Data Products**

We used satellite images from the NASA Landsat program, which captures information in about 30 x 30m grids ("pixels"), to track changes in island vegetation structure and condition through time. From these images, we measured three main aspects of vegetation: Canopy Cover (or Tree Canopy Cover "TCC"), Canopy Moisture ("NDMI"), and Productivity ("NDVI", "EVI", or "MSAVI2").
