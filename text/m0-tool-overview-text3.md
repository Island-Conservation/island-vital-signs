#### Probability Levels

Leaflet maps are interactive. You can choose how strict to set the confidence ("probability level"). When you choose a probability level, you're deciding how sure you want to be before calling a change "real."

**.75: Exploratory:** We're fairly (75%) certain the change is real, but there's a 25% chance that we could be wrong. Useful for spotting early signals that warrant additional investigation.

**.85: Balanced:** We're moderately (85%) certain the change is real, though there's a 15% chance that we could be wrong. Intended to provide enough confidence to trust the results without overlooking emerging patterns.

**.95: Strict:** We are highly (95%) certain the change is real and there's a 5% chance that we could be wrong. Focuses only on the most certain changes.
