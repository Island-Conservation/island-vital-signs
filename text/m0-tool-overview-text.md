Island ecosystems are incredibly vulnerable to biodiversity loss and environmental disruption. However, conservation teams often lack the long-term data needed to fully understand change occurring on individual islands. Invasive mammals, such as rats, pigs, cats, goats, and rabbits, may affect native vegetation through grazing and trampling. They may also drive native species towards extinction. These impacts reduce ecosystem stability, leaving islands vulnerable to drought, erosion, and extreme weather. While removing invasive species is a proven strategy to restore island ecosystems, tracking how an ecosystem recovers over time, and knowing where to focus restoration efforts next, remains a major challenge.

The tool provides detailed, location-specific insights for each island, delivering value to conservation practitioners and decision makers by enabling:

1. Assessment of ecological change in data limited or hard-to-access areas.

2. Prioritization of restoration, rewilding, and monitoring efforts, based on where change is happening.

3. Reporting on ecosystem outcomes tied to vegetation stability, drought resilience, and carbon storage.

### **Core modules in the app:**

#### 1. Annual Values

This module tracks yearly model-predicted values for tree canopy cover, vegetation moisture, and vegetation productivity at up to 30-meter resolution, enabling users to see how vegetation is changing over space and time.

#### 2. Change Over Time

This module depicts accumulated change and annual rates of change in each vegetation metric, helping to identify where restoration is succeeding, where passive recovery is sufficient, and where more action is needed.

#### 3. Classified Change

This module visualizes where a vegetation metric is high, where it is low, where it is increasing, and where it is decreasing, highlighting areas that may be of interest for practitioners and land managers.

#### 4. Management Questions

This module answers practical management questions such as where productivity is high and rising, where productivity is low and falling, where productivity is changing the fastest, where productivity is resisting change, and where forest may be regenerating or declining.

### **Key Terms:**

#### Vegetation Metrics

**Canopy cover (or tree canopy cover, tree cover; "TCC"):** Shows changes in forest structure and extent. Tree canopy cover is the global standard for measuring forest restoration success and indicates resilient ecosystems and carbon storage.

**Canopy Moisture ("NDMI"):** Measures vegetation water content and recovery patterns to identify drought resilience, fire risk, regimes shifts, and areas resilient to climate change. Canopy moisture is a metric that is widely used by climate researchers.

**Productivity ("NDVI," "EVI," or "MSAVI2"):** Captures plant greenness. Vegetation productivity is a gold standard metric for measuring vegetation health and growth and is used in many restoration studies.
